Scrabble Rules:

Scrabble is a word-building board game where players use letter tiles to create words on a 15x15 grid, earning points based on the value of each letter and the placement of the word. Players draw seven tiles at the start of the game and replenish their rack after each turn. Words must connect to existing words on the board and follow standard dictionary spelling. Special board spaces—such as double-letter, triple-letter, double-word, and triple-word scores—multiply the value of placed tiles. A player may exchange tiles instead of playing, but doing so forfeits their turn. The game continues until all tiles are drawn and one player uses all their tiles or no moves remain. The player with the highest total score, including bonuses or deductions from unused tiles, wins the game.
